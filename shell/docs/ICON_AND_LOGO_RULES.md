# OTEKH Icon and Logo Rules

## Supplied identity assets

Use the files in `assets/logos` without redrawing, stretching, tracing, rotating, recolouring outside approved variants, or separating internal elements.

## Logo selection

- Full banner: wide headers, websites, formal splash screens.
- Full stacked logo: covers, installers, about screens.
- Wordmark: narrow persistent application chrome.
- Monochrome mark: one-colour printing and constrained contexts.

Maintain clear space equal to at least 10% of the mark's width. Do not place the full logo inside every panel.

## Application icons

The supplied app icons establish the family:

- olive field,
- black dominant pictogram,
- harvest-gold internal detail,
- OTEKH wordmark as a presentation lockup where shown.

For operating-system icons, prepare a symbol-only crop without the wordmark when the wordmark becomes illegible. Keep the full lockup for promotional tiles and documentation.

## New icon construction

- begin with a single instantly recognizable object or relationship,
- use filled geometry before fine outlines,
- restrict to black plus one gold internal feature on olive,
- preserve generous empty space,
- avoid generic stock-AI brains, robots, sparkles, clouds, and circuit-board decoration,
- avoid faux-Indigenous patterning,
- test in monochrome,
- test at 16, 24, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024 px.

## Export set

Each production icon should ship as:

- master SVG,
- 1024 px PNG,
- 512 px PNG,
- 256 px PNG,
- 128 px PNG,
- 64 px PNG,
- 32 px PNG,
- 16 px PNG,
- macOS `.icns` where required,
- Linux PNG/hicolor set,
- web favicon and PWA manifest assets where required.
